I am a [Prime Minister's Research Fellow (PMRF)](https://pmrf.in/) and a Joint PhD scholar in Mathematics at the [Department of Mathematics, IIT Madras](https://math.iitm.ac.in/) and the [School of Mathematics and Statistics, The University of Melbourne](https://ms.unimelb.edu.au/). Tam](https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/profile/859328-matthew-tam), and I am also a recipient of the [Melbourne Research Scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-research-scholarship).

{: .notice--primary}
**Research keywords.** Operator splitting, primal--dual methods, monotone inclusions, variational inequalities, and convex optimisation.

## Research

My research is centred on structured convex optimisation and monotone operator theory, with a particular emphasis on developing computationally efficient, largely parameter-free, and easily implementable algorithms. I am especially interested in first-order methods that exploit problem structure and remain effective for large-scale models.

More specifically, I work on operator splitting methods, primal--dual algorithms, and monotone inclusion problems, together with applications that connect modern optimisation theory with machine learning and related computational problems.

## Current interests

- Operator splitting methods
- Primal--dual algorithms
- Monotone inclusion problems
- Variational inequalities
- Convex optimisation
