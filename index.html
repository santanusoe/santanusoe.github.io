Santanu Soe

I am a Prime Minister's Research Fellow and a Joint PhD student in Mathematics at IIT Madras and The University of Melbourne. I work under the supervision of V. Vetrivel and Matthew K. Tam, and I am a recipient of the Melbourne Research Scholarship.

Research keywords

Operator splitting methods, primal–dual algorithms, monotone inclusion problems, variational inequalities, and convex optimisation.
