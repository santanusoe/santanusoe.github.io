Mathematics · Optimization · Variational Analysis

Santanu Soe

I am a Prime Minister's Research Fellow and a Joint PhD scholar in Mathematics at IIT Madras and The University of Melbourne.

Doctoral research

Under the supervision of V. Vetrivel and Matthew K. Tam.

Research focus

My work develops practical first-order methods for structured convex optimization and monotone problems, with an emphasis on algorithms that are mathematically transparent, adaptive, and easy to implement.

  • Operator splitting
  • Primal–dual methods
  • Monotone inclusions
  • Variational inequalities
  • Convex optimisation
